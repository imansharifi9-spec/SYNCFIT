# One-Sentence Company Description

> SyncFit is the all-in-one fitness platform where you track workouts and nutrition for free, own your data forever, and get AI or human coaching whenever you're ready.

---

## Pitch Variations

**For gym-goers:**
Track workouts and nutrition in one place — free. Upgrade when you want an AI coach or the right human coach.

**For coaches:**
Get new clients and see their workout and nutrition data in one dashboard — without owning their history.

**For investors:**
SyncFit is the fitness operating system — free tracking, premium AI coaching, and a marketplace where users own their data and coaches earn through discovery.
