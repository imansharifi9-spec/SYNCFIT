# Vision

SyncFit is the all-in-one fitness platform that enables users to track workouts and nutrition for free while providing seamless access to AI coaching and professional fitness coaches.

Unlike existing coaching platforms, users own their fitness data and can choose whether to work independently, use an AI coach, or hire a real coach.

---

# Problem Statement

Today's fitness ecosystem is fragmented.

A user often needs:

* One app for workout tracking
* One app for nutrition tracking
* One app for communicating with a coach
* One app for progress photos
* Spreadsheets or PDFs for weekly check-ins

This creates friction for both users and coaches.

SyncFit eliminates this fragmentation by creating a single platform where every aspect of a user's fitness journey is connected.

---

# Mission

**Become the operating system for personal fitness.**

One account. One fitness profile. Unlimited coaching possibilities.

# Business Model

## Free Tier

Users can:

* Log workouts
* Track calories and macronutrients
* Track protein
* Track body weight
* Upload progress photos
* View basic analytics and charts

## SyncFit+ ($9.99/month)

Unlocks:

* AI Coach
* Coach Marketplace
* Coach messaging
* Coach dashboard access
* AI weekly reports
* Advanced analytics
* Personalized recommendations
* Future premium AI features

## Coach Marketplace

Users can browse verified coaches. Each coach determines their own pricing.

SyncFit facilitates discovery, messaging, payments, progress tracking, and data sharing.

Revenue:

* 10% commission on coaching subscriptions
* Future premium software plans for coaches

---

# Core Philosophy

**The user owns their fitness data.**

Users can hire Coach A, remove Coach A, hire Coach B, and maintain their entire workout history, nutrition logs, progress photos, analytics, and achievements.

---

# Core Features

## Workout Tracking

Exercise library, sets, reps, weight, RPE, personal records, workout history, custom routines.

## Nutrition Tracking

Food database, barcode scanner, calories, protein, carbs, fat, water intake, meal history.

## Progress Tracking

Weight, body measurements, progress photos, workout consistency, nutrition adherence, strength progression.

## AI Coach

Analyzes goals, workout history, nutrition, weight trends. Provides recommendations, weekly reports, plateau detection, accountability.

## Coach Marketplace

Search by goal, budget, specialty, online/in-person, location, language, rating, availability.

## Coach Dashboard

Client management, adherence tracking, messaging, workout programming, macro targets, AI-generated client summaries.

## Messaging

Coach ↔ Client communication (text, images, documents).
