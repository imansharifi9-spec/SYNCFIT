# Weekly Founder Meeting Template

**Cadence:** Every week  
**Duration:** 60 minutes recommended

---

## Agenda

1. **Previous week's commitments** (10 min)  
   Review each assigned task: done / blocked / rescheduled?

2. **User interview findings** (10 min)  
   Mateen shares top insights from gym-goer interviews.

3. **Coach interview findings** (10 min)  
   Iman shares top insights from coach interviews.

4. **Product development progress** (10 min)  
   Prototype, design, or dev updates.

5. **Problems surfaced** (5 min)  
   User or coach issues that need a decision.

6. **Spending and budget** (5 min)  
   Expenses this week, remaining budget.

7. **Key metrics** (5 min)  
   Interviews completed, waitlist, coaches recruited.

8. **Decisions requiring both founders** (5 min)

9. **Tasks for next 7 days** (10 min)  
   Each task: owner, deadline, measurable result.

## Task Quality Rule

❌ Bad: "Work on marketing"  
✅ Good: "Mateen will interview 5 gym-goers and summarize the top 3 repeated complaints by Friday."

Every task needs: **one owner**, **one deadline**, **one measurable result**.
