## Budget Rules

- Do not spend heavily before completing interviews
- Do not pay entire development cost upfront
- Use milestone-based payments for all dev work
- Require ownership of source code and design files
- Require written documentation from contractors
- Maintain access to all company accounts
- Keep 20% of dev budget as contingency
- No large ad campaigns before retention is proven
