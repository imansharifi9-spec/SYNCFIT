### Expensive to Reproduce (Don't Build First)

- Large proprietary food databases
- ML form analysis
- Wearable integrations at scale

### SyncFit Differentiation

| Differentiator | Why It Matters |
|----------------|----------------|
| User owns data | Switch coaches without losing history |
| Free core tracking | Low barrier to adoption |
| Optional coaching | Not forced into coach relationship |
| Unified data for coaches | One dashboard vs. spreadsheets |
| AI + human in one place | Upgrade path when user hits plateau |
