# Coach Interview Guide

**Target:** 15 online coaches + 10 in-person trainers  
**Owner:** Iman  
**Rule:** Do not pitch SyncFit first. Understand their current workflow.

---

## Opening

> Thanks for your time. I'm researching how coaches manage clients — tracking, communication, finding new clients. I want to understand your real workflow, not sell you anything.

---

## Core Questions

1. How do you find new clients today?
2. What software or tools do you use to manage clients?
3. How do clients submit nutrition logs to you?
4. How do clients submit workout data?
5. How much time do you spend reviewing each client's progress per week?
6. What information do clients frequently forget to submit?
7. What do you dislike most about current coaching platforms?
8. Would you use a platform that brings you new clients?
9. Would a 10% commission on clients acquired through the platform be acceptable?
10. Would you invite existing clients to use a new platform if it made your job easier?
11. What information would you need on a client dashboard?
12. What would make you distrust a coaching marketplace?

---

## Follow-Up Probes

- "Walk me through your last client check-in."
- "What's the most annoying part of your current setup?"
- "Have clients ever left because of your tools?"
- "What would make you recommend a platform to other coaches?"

---

## After the Interview (Only If Relevant)

Briefly describe SyncFit and ask:

- Would this save you time?
- Would you join as a founding coach during beta?
- What pricing would you set?
- What's your biggest concern?

---

## Track Across All Interviews

- [ ] Current tools used (frequency count)
- [ ] Top 3 workflow pain points
- [ ] Commission acceptance (yes / negotiable / no)
- [ ] Founding coach interest (yes / maybe / no)
- [ ] Willingness to invite existing clients
