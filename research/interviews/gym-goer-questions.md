# Gym-Goer Interview Guide

**Target:** 25 interviews minimum  
**Owner:** Mateen  
**Rule:** Do not explain SyncFit first. Learn how they currently behave.

---

## Opening

> Thanks for taking the time. I'm researching how people track their fitness — workouts, nutrition, progress. There are no right or wrong answers. I'd love to hear about your actual experience.

---

## Core Questions

1. How do you currently track workouts?
2. How do you currently track nutrition?
3. How many fitness apps do you use regularly?
4. What frustrates you most about those apps?
5. Have you ever worked with a coach (online or in-person)?
6. If yes — how did you find them? If no — why not?
7. What stopped you from hiring a coach sooner (or what would)?
8. Would you want a coach to see your workout and nutrition logs?
9. Would you use an AI coach? What would make it feel trustworthy?
10. Would you pay $9.99/month for AI coaching and access to a coach marketplace?
11. Would you prefer an online coach or a local in-person coach?
12. If you switched to a new fitness app, what would you need to see in the first week to keep using it?

---

## Follow-Up Probes

- "Can you walk me through your last workout — how did you log it?"
- "What happened the last time you stopped using a fitness app?"
- "What data would you never want a coach to see?"
- "What would make you delete an app immediately?"

---

## After the Interview (Only If Relevant)

Briefly describe SyncFit concept and ask:

- Does this solve a problem you actually have?
- What's missing?
- Would you try a beta?
- Can we follow up?
