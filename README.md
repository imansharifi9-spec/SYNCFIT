# SyncFit

**Track your fitness for free. Get guidance when you need it.**

SyncFit is the all-in-one fitness platform where users own their data and can track workouts and nutrition for free, then upgrade to SyncFit+ for AI coaching and access to verified human coaches.

## Current Phase

**Phase 1: Foundation** (Week 1)

The first milestone is not launching the application. It is proving that a clearly defined group of users and coaches has a problem important enough for SyncFit to solve.

Do not move to MVP development until validation gates are met (see `docs/execution-plan.md`).

## Validation Gates (Before MVP Development)

- 60%+ of gym-goers use multiple tools or dislike current setup
- 40%+ express serious interest in a combined tracker
- 25%+ willing to consider $9.99/month subscription
- 10+ coaches interested in testing the platform
- Most coaches consider 10% acquisition commission reasonable
