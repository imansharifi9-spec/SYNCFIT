# Features Excluded From MVP

These are in the PRD or execution plan but **must not** be built in Version 1.

---

## AI & Advanced Intelligence

- AI Coach (full conversational)
- AI meal analysis / food recognition
- AI weekly reports
- Video form analysis
- Plateau detection algorithms

**Rationale:** Version 2 feature. Requires validated data model and safety testing.

---

## Integrations

- Apple Health
- Google Health Connect
- Wearable devices (Whoop, Garmin, etc.)
- Barcode scanner (evaluate food API first; may add in v1.1 if provider supports it)

---

## Social & Community

- Community feeds
- Leaderboards
- Challenges
- Groups

---

## Communication (Advanced)

- Voice messages
- Video messages
- Video consultations / calls
- Appointment scheduling

---

## Marketplace (Advanced)

- Complex coach ranking algorithms
- Featured coach placements (paid)
- International expansion
- Verified review system (basic ratings OK for MVP)
- Local trainer map view

---

## Commerce

- Supplement marketplace
- Affiliate marketplace
- Meal delivery partnerships
- Educational courses

---

## Coach Tools (Advanced)

- Advanced workout builder (drag-and-drop periodization)
- Coach subscription SaaS plans
- Bulk client programming
- Automated check-in forms

---

## When to Revisit

Each excluded feature should be reconsidered only when supported by:

- User interview data
- Beta usage metrics
- Revenue potential
- Operational necessity

Do not add features during beta unless they fix a critical blocker.
